💡Stop Loss Guide
% Based Stop:
Places SL at X% from entry. Simple but doesn't account for volatility.

ATR Based Stop (1.5× ATR):
Uses Average True Range to place SL beyond normal noise. Best for volatile stocks.

Rule: Always place SL before calculating position size.

Tip: For NSE stocks, use 1D ATR. For intraday, use 15m ATR.
